1 Problem Formulation

The Challenge: Intraday crypto markets have extreme noise and transaction cost frictions. Traditional technical indicators (RSI, MACD) apply static heuristics, cannot learn non-linear patterns, and often repaint.

Formal Task: Binary direction prediction ($y \in \{\text{Down}, \text{Up}\}$) 4 candles ($1\text{ hour}$) ahead on SOLUSDT with an explicit abstention dead-zone ($|\text{return}| \le 0.20\%$).

3 End-to-End System Workflow

Strict chronological separation with an embargo of $H=4$ candles eliminates all look-ahead bias and serial correlation leakage:

Platt Probability Calibration: $$P(\text{Up}) = \frac{1}{1 + \exp(-(0.253 \cdot \text{logit}(p) + 0.020))}$$ Fitted on 29,566 out-of-fold predictions. A reported $55\%$ confidence guarantees that historical accuracy matches $\approx 55\%$.

Subgroup Performance:

  • US Market Hours (18–23 UTC): Peak accuracy ($59.6\%$).
  • Asian/EU Morning (06–11 UTC): Lowest accuracy ($49.9\%$).
  • Failure Modes: Losses concentrate around macro flash-crash cascades where technicals decouple from order books.
